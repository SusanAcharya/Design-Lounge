Method

Six weeks, three phases
Slow is a decision. We listen for three weeks, then make it look easy.
  1. i.

    Listen

    Three weeks of interviews, archives and site visits before a single sketch.

  2. ii.

    Draw

    Marks, type and motion rules drawn together, tested at 16 pixels and at 16 metres.

  3. iii.

    Hand over

    A system your team runs without us: source files, motion rules and a 40-page manual.
