Case file 02 / Below the line

The tide
gives things
back.

The schooner Marrow Bell went down in 1887 with a cargo of church bells. On still nights at low water, fishermen swear they hear one.

Case file 03 / Over the headland

Three lights.
No boat.
No answer.

Since 1962 the coastguard has logged the same three lights over Crane Head forty-one times. The walk ends where you can see the spot.
