    02

    Chapter two
    The starter

    Agnes lives in a jar

    Our starter is forty-one years old. She is fed rye and well water at 6:00 and 18:00, and she has never left the island.

    03

    Chapter three
    The mill

    Stone-milled on Tuesdays

    Rye from Holm Farm, two fields from the bakery. We mill 180 kg a week and stop when it runs out.

    04

    Chapter four
    The fold

    Four folds, by hand

    Thirty minutes apart, in a room kept at 24°C. No mixer touches the dough after the first ten minutes.

    05

    Chapter five
    The oven

    250°C, then 210°C

    Fifty-five minutes on stone. The crust should crackle for a full minute after it leaves the oven. If it does not, it stays here.

    On the 7:40 ferry

    Order by Thursday 18:00. Collect at Skerry pier, or we bring it to your door in Arnhaven.
