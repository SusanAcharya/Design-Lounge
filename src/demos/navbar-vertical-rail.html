02 About

Two people, one Riso.

Ruma Office is Anju Shrestha and Pablo Viñals. We have run a GR3750 duplicator out of a courtyard in Patan since 2022, printing in pink, black and whatever drum we can borrow.

We take four commissions a season: identity, books and signage. Everything we design is proofed on paper the same afternoon, so clients see ink, not a screen.
