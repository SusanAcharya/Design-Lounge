The case for slow correspondence

Why a letter that takes a week to arrive still says more than a hundred quick replies.

By Margit Sallow · 12 min read · 3 Oct 2026
Email to a friendOpens your mail app Send as a textTitle and link, nothing else

The postcard came eleven days after it was sent. By then the weather it described had passed, the friend who wrote it had moved flats, and none of that mattered. What arrived was attention: a person who had chosen, on a Tuesday, to spend a stamp and a quarter of an hour on me.
