The last reservoir: how a valley learned to ration its own river

Three summers of drought forced the Sørvik basin to invent a water market. Now other regions want the ledger, not the water.

By Ida Rønning
Photographs by Tomas Ekelund
18 min read

Every Tuesday at six, Anders Holm walks the 400 metres from his farmhouse to the sluice and reads a number off a brass plate. He has done this since 1989. The number used to tell him how much water was left. Now it tells him what a cubic metre costs.
