One cell
Everything is 1ch wide and 22px tall. Gaps are 1ch or 2ch, never 10px.

    
95 glyphs
Printable ASCII only. Web font subsets drop box drawing, and one fallback glyph tears a frame.

    
Brackets act
Text in [ brackets ] is a button. Parentheses pick one, square marks pick many.

    
One signal
Cyan means act or selected. Amber and red only report money.
