Issue 14 · Early autumn

The Allotment Letter

Plot 22 is giving up its last beans, the shed roof finally holds, and Nell has opinions about leeks.

We cleared the north beds on Saturday and found the onions we lost in June, small, sweet and entirely intact. Tomas wants them pickled. The committee wants them on the swap table.

The seed library reopens on the 12th. Bring envelopes, labels and anything that came true from last year's saved heads of chard.

Water butts get drained before the first frost. Rhea will post the rota on the gate.
