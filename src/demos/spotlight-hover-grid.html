Platform · v2.14

Edge routing

Requests land on the nearest of 34 regions, with failover in 200 ms.

Zero-config TLS

Certificates issued at first deploy and rotated 30 days before expiry.

Preview deploys

Every branch gets its own URL and a copy-on-write fork of the database.

Query cache

Repeated reads answered in 4 ms from the edge; invalidated on write.

Audit log

Every change signed by its author and searchable for 400 days.

Cron jobs

Schedules in your timezone, three retries with backoff, logs per run.

Secrets

Scoped per environment, encrypted at rest, never written to your repo.

Metrics

p50, p95 and p99 per route, without installing an agent.

Rollbacks

One click back to any of the last 50 builds. Median rollback: 9 s.

Team roles

Owner, deployer, viewer. That is the whole permissions model.

Webhooks

Signed payloads, delivery receipts, and a 7-day replay window.

SSO

SAML and OIDC on every plan, including the free one.
