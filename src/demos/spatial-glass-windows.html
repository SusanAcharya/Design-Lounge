The salt roads of Aveiro

Tomás Leitão Rocha · 11 October 2026

By four in the afternoon the pans have gone the colour of weak tea, and Dona Albertina is still raking. She works the crystals toward the edge in long strokes, never twice over the same water.

Her grandmother kept sixty pans on this flat. Today there are nine, and a sign the council put up in 2019 that the wind has already half erased.
