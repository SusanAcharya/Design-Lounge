The harbour at dusk
Post card

Dear Ama,

The ferry runs twice a day, so the whole town keeps time by it. I swim before breakfast, eat sardines on the harbour wall, and fall asleep to the lighthouse sweeping my ceiling.

Saving you a jar of the lemon honey.

back Thursday, R.

Ama Thapa 14 Juniper Row Harrow Fold  HF3 9QL
