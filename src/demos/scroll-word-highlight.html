02About the bindery

Fenwick Bindery · Leith, since 1987

We bind books the slow way. Every signature is folded by hand, sewn on linen tape and left under the press overnight, because glue dries in an hour but a spine has to last a lifetime. We do not chase the season. We keep one bench, three people and a shelf of waxed thread, and we would rather make forty books that open flat than four thousand that crack.
