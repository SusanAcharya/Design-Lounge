Questions, answered

Is the 4.35% rate fixed?

No. It is variable and tracks our base rate. We give 30 days' notice before any decrease, and increases apply the same day.

Can I withdraw whenever I want?

Yes. Transfers to your linked current account arrive in under two hours, 24 hours a day, with no notice period or penalty.

How do round-ups work with shared cards?

Each cardholder links their own card. Round-ups from that card go to the pot they choose, so nobody funds someone else's holiday by accident.

What does Loam cost?

Nothing. There is no monthly fee and no minimum balance. We earn a margin on deposits, which is disclosed in the account terms.
