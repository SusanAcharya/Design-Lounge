    For parents

    Questions parents ask us

    Is there a cut-off mark for the entrance test?

    No single cut-off. We read the test, the school report, the reference, and the interview together. Most offers go to students above 60 percent in the test.

    Can my child switch streams after starting?

    Yes, within the first six weeks of Grade 11, if a place is free in the new stream. After that we advise against it.

    How is need-based aid decided?

    A separate panel reviews family income and circumstances after offers are made. Aid can cover up to 80 percent of fees and is renewed each year.

    Do you run a school bus?

    Five routes cover the valley, leaving between 6:40 and 7:10. The bus costs NPR 4,500 a month.

    What do day students do for lunch?

    Lunch is included in the fees and served in the dining hall at 12:40. Vegetarian and allergy-safe plates are always on.
