Reading room and the north light

The council has agreed to keep the 1931 reading room. Our new wing wraps it on two sides and must not take its north light. Plan for 40,000 volumes, a children's floor with its own entrance, and a hall for 120 that can stay open after the library closes.

Budget is fixed. Programme is not, so long as we open before the school year in 2028.
