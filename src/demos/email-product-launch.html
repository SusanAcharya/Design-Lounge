What changed

Most reviews are not one pull request. They are four, built on top of each other, by someone who wants to go home. Lathe 3.0 is built for that.

Stacked reviews

Open a stack of up to 12 dependent pull requests as one review. Approve them together or one at a time; Lathe rebases the rest.

Review from your phone

Real diffs, threaded comments and approvals in the new app. Swipe right to approve, left to request changes.

Faster by half

A 4,000-line diff now opens in 0.4 seconds, down from 0.9. Syntax highlighting no longer blocks scrolling.
