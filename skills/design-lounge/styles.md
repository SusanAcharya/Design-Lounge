# Styles

Read this only when they named a style. Find their word in the table, write `Named aesthetic: <style> · scope <page|hero>` in the brief, and route as the row says. A row that names a section of [taste.md](taste.md) (*Industrial brutalist*, *Minimal editorial*, *Games and Three.js*, *Glass*, *High-end agency*) hands over to that section for the method. A style that resolves to a row lifts the bans on its signature elements: Named aesthetic in [practice.md](practice.md).
