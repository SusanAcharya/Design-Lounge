One week,
three small moves.

Pocketplan is built around the way a small team already works: someone plans on Friday, everyone looks on Monday, and a few reminders keep the middle of the week honest.

  1. Friday afternoon

    Plan

    Drag next week's tasks out of the backlog and onto days. Give each one an owner. Repeats drop in by themselves.
