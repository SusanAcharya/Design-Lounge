The rule

Kathmandu goes quiet after midnight. The traffic thins, the dogs settle, and the valley holds its breath until the first bell. We release only what is recorded in that quiet: between 00:00 and sunrise, Nepal time, with the file's own timestamp as the proof. No overdubs after breakfast. No mix made at noon.
