Layout

Sixty-five percent.
Nothing you’d miss.

Letters, numbers and symbols, a full modifier row, arrow keys, and Delete, Page Up, Page Down and End down the right-hand edge. 68 keys in all. Tap Fn on the board to see the function layer on the number row.

  • Your switchLinear, Tactile or Clicky, chosen when you order. The board above runs on whichever you pick in the switch control.
  • Function layerHold Fn and the number row becomes F1 to F12. Try it on the board: tap Fn to lock the layer, tap again to release.
  • Sculpted capsFive rows, four angles. The number row leans back, the home row sits flat, the space row leans in.
  • Standard sizes1.25-unit modifiers, a 6.25-unit space bar, a 2-unit Backspace: the sizes keycap sets are made in, so the caps are easy to swap.
