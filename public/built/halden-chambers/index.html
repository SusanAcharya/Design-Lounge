Approach

How a matter usually runs.

Every matter is different. Most follow the same four stages, and you will always know which one you are in.

Read

A partner reads what you send, then calls you before anyone else is involved.

Advise

A short written view: how strong your position is, what it may cost, and the routes that avoid court.

Act

Letters, negotiation or proceedings, run by the same partner from the first page to the last.

Close

A settlement, a judgment or an award, with a plain account of what it cost against the estimate.

This is the usual shape of a matter. It is not a promise of any outcome.

Principles

Halden Chambers

We are a small firm on purpose. Every matter is run by a partner who reads the documents themselves, answers the telephone, and tells you early whether the case is worth fighting. We would rather settle a dispute well than prolong it for fees. When it must go to court, we prepare as though the judge will read every page, because sometimes they do.
