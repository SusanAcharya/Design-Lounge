Four layers under every press.

One key, cut through the middle. Press each drawing and watch what moves.

  1. Cap

    The part your finger meets and the part you read. On the board above, the caps you press last become the name.

  2. Switch

    One switch under each cap. The stem drops, the spring gives, two contacts meet, and the key counts.

  3. Plate

    The plate holds every switch at one height, so a row of sixty-one caps reads as one flat surface.

  4. Case

    The case closes it all. It is what the desk sees, and the shell every press sounds inside.
