Late Light

A late-night show under the planetarium dome. Scroll, and you leave your seat: past the Sun, the nearest star and the middle of the Milky Way, out to Andromeda.

I / III Moonrise The Moon clears the dome.

The Sun

Eight minutes old.

Sunlight takes 8 min 19 s to cross 149.6 million km. You just did it in one scroll.

Proxima Centauri

4.24 years of light.

The nearest star after the Sun is a small red one, too faint for your eyes. The light you would see from it set out 4.24 years ago.

The Milky Way

26,000 years to the middle.

Our galaxy is about 100,000 light years across. The Sun is the small ring on the left, in a quiet arm, a long way from the bright centre.

Andromeda

The oldest light your eyes can find.

2.5 million light years away. On a dark night it is the farthest thing most people can see without a telescope. The show takes you there on the dome.
