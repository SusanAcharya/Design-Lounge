Built in four layers.

Every part between your finger and the desk does one job. Scroll through them, or press a number.

  1. Milled

    The case is one block of 6063 aluminium, CNC milled and bead-blasted, with a 6.5° typing angle cut into the shape.

    • 1.62 kgweight
    • 6.5°typing angle

  2. Cushioned

    Twelve Poron gaskets hold a 1.5 mm FR4 plate away from the metal, so each press lands soft and the case does not ring.

    • 12gaskets
    • 1.5 mmFR4 plate

  3. Switched

    A hot-swap board takes any 3 or 5-pin switch. Pull one out with the included tool and push another in. No solder.

    • 84sockets
    • 3factory switches

  4. Capped

    Double-shot PBT keycaps in Cherry profile. Each legend is a second plastic moulded through the cap, so it cannot wear off.

    • 1.5 mmcap wall
    • PBTdouble-shot

Three switches. Hear each one.

Linear 45 gf

Smooth from top to bottom with no bump. The quietest of the three, and the one to pick for long sessions or a shared room.

Tactile 55 gf

A small bump at 2 mm tells your finger the key has registered before it bottoms out. The middle ground, and how KEY-01 ships unless you choose.

Clicky 60 gf

A click jacket snaps at the actuation point. You hear it on the way down and again on the way up. Loud on purpose.

Every sound on this page is synthesised in your browser from these three switch profiles. Your speakers colour it, so treat it as a sketch of the real thing.
