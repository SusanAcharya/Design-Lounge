Four studies,
drawn to brief.

Each one is a self-set brief: a real kind of problem, drawn the way we would answer it. They are studies, not client work.

Study: a wayfinding system for a night market

Signs that read at a glance under string lights, with one letter per aisle.

Study: a wordmark for a community radio station

Two letters that survive a sticker, a mic flag and a phone screen.

Study: a display typeface for a bike repair co-op

Round counters taken from the wheel, cut square where the spokes meet.

Study: labels for a small-batch hot sauce

One word, one colour, printed by hand. The heat is in the type, not a chilli icon.
