Four moves.
Forty-four minutes.

  1. Warm up

    Six minutes to find your cadence. The lights come down as the room gets going.

  2. Climb

    Heavy gear, slow legs, out of the saddle when the coach calls it. Each climb builds in steps you can see on the profile.

  3. Sprint

    Short and flat out. Twenty seconds hard, then a rest you will be glad of, then again.

  4. Recover

    The last six minutes bring your heart rate down before you clip out. Lights up, towels out.
