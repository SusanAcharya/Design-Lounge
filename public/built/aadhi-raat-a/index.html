The rule

Kathmandu, after 00:00

Aadhi Raat means half the night. This label releases one kind of music: music recorded after midnight in Kathmandu. Not written after midnight, not mixed after midnight. Recorded, once the clocks in the valley pass twelve. A take that starts at 23:59 waits for another night. What you hear here was caught in the hours when the city had gone quiet.
