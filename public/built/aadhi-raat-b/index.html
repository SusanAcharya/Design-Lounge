Nothing on this label was recorded before midnight.

Aadhi raat means midnight. It is also the whole idea. We record in Kathmandu once the city goes quiet: the dogs settle, the Ring Road empties, and the valley starts to sound like itself. A take has to start after 00:00 or it is not ours. The session ends at the first temple bell. What you hear on every release is the night, kept.

Every take is filed under the minute the tape rolled.
