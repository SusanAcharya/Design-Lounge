A silent retreat above Pokhara

Thulo Dhunga

Three, seven or ten nights without speaking, in a stone house on the ridge above Phewa lake.

Choose arrival dates See one day

A stone house, a ridge, and no words for a while.

Thulo Dhunga means big stone. The house is built against one, 1,600 m up the Sarangkot ridge, with the Annapurna range to the north and Phewa lake below.

You arrive on a Thursday, hand your phone to the office, and keep silence until the last morning. Meals, sitting, walking and work are laid out for you, so there is nothing to decide.

The lake does the talking.

One day, the same every day.

The bell keeps time so you do not have to. Nobody checks that you go to every sitting. Most people find that they want to by the third morning.

  1. 05:30

    First bell

    Tea on the terrace while the Annapurna range turns pink.

  2. 06:00

    Sitting

    Ninety minutes in the hall, with a short walk in the middle.

  3. 07:30

    Porridge and fruit

    Eaten slowly, facing the lake, without a phone to look at.

  4. 09:00

    Walking the ridge

    One path, marked with white stones, two hours there and back.

  5. 12:00

    Dal bhat

    Rice, lentils, greens from the terraces. Second helpings are brought without asking.

  6. 14:00

    Work period

    An hour in the garden or the kitchen. Hands busy, mouth closed.

  7. 16:30

    Sitting

    The afternoon session, as the cloud lifts off the valley.

  8. 19:30

    Lamp out

    Soup at six, a short reading at seven, then the house goes dark.

Choose the Thursday you arrive.

Every session begins on a Thursday at 15:00. Days in grey are closed or already full. A hold keeps your place for 48 hours while you pay the deposit.
