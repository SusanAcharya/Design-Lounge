# Ledgerly

## Brief

Request, word for word:
"Build a landing page for Ledgerly, invoicing software for small accounting firms. It should make people trust it with their clients' money."

Product: Ledgerly, invoicing software for small accounting firms.
For: a partner or practice manager at a small firm, who wants to see in ten seconds that invoices are exact, checked and traceable before client money moves.
World: ledger paper, a partner's initials, the bank statement.
The one thing: the invoice itself, drafted live on the page, with every change written into a log beside it.
Ambition: finished, because "make people trust it with their clients' money"
Mode: Kit
Named aesthetic: none
Stack: plain index.html + styles.css + main.js that works when opened directly
Missing: pricing, trial terms, payment provider, security certifications, data regions, customers and quotes, sign-in URL, contact address. The sample invoice data in the hero is marked as sample data.

Defaults set aside: the tabs' giant numerals, the footer's column numerals, status pill and certification row are dropped (numbered eyebrows and invented facts).
