How it works

One invoice, from draft to a closed month.

Follow INV-1042 through Ledgerly. The card on the right is the same invoice at each stage.

  1. Draft

    Build the invoice from the engagement: line items, tax and payment terms. Nothing leaves the firm until someone sends it.

    • Line items from the engagement
    • Terms set per client

  2. Send

    The client gets a page they can read and pay from. You see when it was opened, and late invoices get a reminder on the schedule you set.

    • Opened and paid dates
    • Reminders you schedule

  3. Match

    When the deposit lands in the bank feed, Ledgerly matches it to the invoice on amount, date and payer. Anything it cannot match waits for a person.

    • Matched on three fields
    • Unmatched deposits queue for review

  4. Close

    At month end, one person closes the period and a second person reviews it. After that, the month is locked and every line keeps its history.

    • Preparer and reviewer on record
    • Closed months are read only

Built for money that is not yours.

Your clients pay you so you can pay others on their behalf. Ledgerly is set up so that money is always in a column you can point to.
