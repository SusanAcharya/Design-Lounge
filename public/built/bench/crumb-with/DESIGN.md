# Crumb & Co

## Brief

Request, word for word:
"Make a mobile-first website for Crumb & Co, a neighbourhood bakery in Lisbon, where people can see today's bakes and pre-order for pickup."

Product: Crumb & Co, a neighbourhood bakery in Lisbon. People see today's bakes and pre-order them for pickup.
For: a neighbour on a phone, on the way out the door, who wants to know what is still on the shelf and keep some under their name.
World: flour paper, oven trays, a Lisbon shop counter in the morning.
The one thing: the handwritten list on the shop door, and a paper pickup slip that fills in as you choose.
Ambition: finished, because "a mobile-first website ... where people can see today's bakes and pre-order"
Mode: Kit
Named aesthetic: none
Stack: plain index.html + styles.css + main.js that works when opened directly
Missing: the real bake list, prices, stock counts, opening hours, street address, phone and email. The page uses stand-ins for these (marked in main.js `BAKES` and in the Visit section) that must be replaced before launch.

## Sheet

- Recipe: food. Direction: pantry-letter (Crumb & Co = 75, 2 open directions, 75 mod 2 = 1)
- Theme: inkwell https://www.designlounge.live/themes/inkwell
- Pairing: letter-hand https://www.designlounge.live/type/letter-hand (Gochi Hand titles, Patrick Hand everything read)
- Family: soft, 14px cards, 999px chips and primary button
- Hero: handwritten-homepage (shape: object, the letter on the door)
- Effect: text-annotated-underlines (marks drawn on the letter)
- Work: features-alternating-rows (one row per oven batch)
- Footer: footer-sitemap-columns
- Frame: navbar-split-centered-logo, mobile-web-bottom-nav, contact-booking-hours
- Idea: the homepage is the note taped to the bakery door, written this morning, with what came out of the oven and what is already gone.
- Signature: the pickup slip. Each bake you add is written onto a ruled paper slip; when you reserve, the stub along the perforation tears off and becomes your claim ticket.
- Avoiding: a cream hero with a croissant photo, three icon cards (Fresh, Local, Handmade), and a cart drawer.
- Default set aside: the pairing warns against form-heavy tools. The order form has three fields and time chips, all in Patrick Hand at 18px or more, so it stays a note, not a tool.
