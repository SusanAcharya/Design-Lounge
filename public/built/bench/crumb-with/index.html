          When can I collect?

          From thirty minutes after you reserve, and never before the tray is out of the oven. Loaves from 12:00, sweets from 16:00.

          Do I pay when I reserve?

          No. You pay at the counter when you collect, by card or cash.

          What if I am late?

          Your bag waits on the shelf for thirty minutes past your time. After that it goes back on the counter.
